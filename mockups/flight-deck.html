The shifter. A vertical gate runs down the left of the accounts with one notch per account and a metallic knob parked in the active gear. Click any notch — or grab the knob and drag — and it chunks down gate by gate with a small settle at each stop, then that account lights up ACTIVE and the menu-bar title follows. A re-auth account is a hatched, locked gate: the knob refuses and springs back. One control, one meaning, its own lane — nothing overlaps the data.
