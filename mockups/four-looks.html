LookChannel strip. A four-channel mixer — one strip per account, side by side, brushed-metal knobs on a charcoal rack. TypeRajdhani (squared, engineering-plate feel) for scribble-strip labels · JetBrains Mono for values. BarsVertical fader = weekly (the knob sits at your level), 12-step LED meter = session, thin pip = model bucket. The active account's scribble strip and button light green. Good atScanning across. Four verticals next to each other is the fastest way to see "the lowest fader is the one I switch to" — and it's the shortest panel of the four. Weakest at reset times, which are relegated to small text.

alexa and Lilabot values are live from your accounts; derek and john use sample numbers (they haven't reported usage yet) so every look shows the yellow and red states. Any of these can be rebuilt as the SwiftUI panel — the Gantt and bar mechanics port directly to Canvas.
