03 · The meter

One row per account. Read it at a glance.

Session (5H), weekly (7D), and the Fable row: Fable gets its own weekly bucket, so it gets its own line. Each one is a 24-segment ladder, a percentage, and a countdown to the reset. Polled every 60 seconds from Anthropic's own usage endpoint, so the numbers are the numbers.

  • UNDER 50%Grey. A fuel gauge you don't look at.
  • 50 TO 79%Amber. Half a tank. Plan the next stop.
  • 80% AND UPRed, and the last segment breathes. Time to switch.

Hover a countdown and it says the reset in words a human uses. "Today at 8:10 PM", not a Unix timestamp.
